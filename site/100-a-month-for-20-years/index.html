What $100 a month could grow to

At an example rate of 5% compounded monthly, $100 added at the end of each month, with no starting balance, would grow to $15,528.23 after 10 years, $41,103.37 after 20 and $83,225.86 after 30. For illustration only. Real returns vary and are not guaranteed. For education only, not financial advice.

At the same example rate of 5% compounded monthly, with $100 added at the end of each month and no starting balance, the 20-year figure is $41,103.37. Of that, $24,000.00 is money you put in (240 payments of $100) and $17,103.37 is interest. Going from 10 to 20 years doubles the time, but the balance is more than two and a half times as large, because the later years earn interest on a bigger balance. For illustration only. Real returns vary and are not guaranteed. For education only, not financial advice.
