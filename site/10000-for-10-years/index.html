What $10,000 could grow to

With $10,000 to start, no monthly additions and interest compounded monthly, an example rate of 5% would grow it to $16,470.09 after 10 years, of which $6,470.09 is interest. For illustration only. Real returns vary and are not guaranteed. For education only, not financial advice.

At an example rate of 7%, the same $10,000 with no monthly additions, compounded monthly, would grow to $40,387.39 after 20 years, of which $30,387.39 is interest. The balance is more than four times the amount put in. For illustration only. Real returns vary and are not guaranteed. For education only, not financial advice.

Both examples assume nothing is added or taken out. To add money each month, enter a monthly contribution above.
